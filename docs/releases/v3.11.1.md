# Frameinsight 3.11.1

## Track controls and camera playback fixes

- Add an eye and trash control beside each local track in multi-camera sessions. Hide complete boxes and labels, or show all boxes in the active camera.
- Keep hidden tracks hidden when stepping frames, switching cameras and reopening the session. Explicitly selecting a local track shows it for editing.
- Confirm deletion of a camera track, including all its classes and frames. Other cameras and the shared person stay intact. Undo delete or Ctrl+Z restores its annotations and shared-person assignment.
- Wait for visible camera frames to load before advancing playback. Slow frame responses no longer cause the player to repeatedly discard previews before they can appear. Playback may slow below the chosen speed when loading cannot keep up.
- Bound the editor’s decoded-image cache by memory and entry count, deduplicate requests and reduce adjacent-frame prefetching.
- Add Retry frame for failed previews in both the annotation canvas and passive camera views, without reloading the page.

Single-video track controls remain available in the Tracks panel. Hiding labels in Canvas settings only changes labels; use a track’s eye to hide its boxes too. Display hiding never removes annotations from exports.

## Update

Save your work and fully exit Frameinsight, then reopen it while online. On Windows, use the tray icon’s **Exit Frameinsight** command; closing the browser alone leaves the app running. Installed versions 3.10.2 and later check for updates at startup. You can also install the official package over your existing copy; saved projects remain in their data folder.
