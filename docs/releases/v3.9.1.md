# Frameinsight 3.9.1

Select a box to see its **width × height in source pixels** at the bottom right of the canvas. The dimensions now stay visible after releasing the mouse; dragging is no longer required.

- Measurements follow the selected track and class, update during edits and frame navigation, and stay independent of zoom.
- Hidden or absent boxes do not leave a stale measurement on screen.
- The dimensions setting has been removed: the readout works automatically, even if an older browser saved that setting as off. Other canvas preferences and all saved annotations remain unchanged.

Includes all 3.9.0 JSON replacement and Debian updater fixes. Install over your current version and reopen Frameinsight. Older Linux versions whose updater fails may need one manual installation of the `.deb`; saved projects are preserved.
