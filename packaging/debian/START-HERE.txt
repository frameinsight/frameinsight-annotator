FRAMEINSIGHT 3.0.1 — UBUNTU / DEBIAN (64-bit Intel/AMD)

Ubuntu 24.04 or newer, or Debian 12 or newer, with a graphical desktop.
Python and video decoding libraries are included. Node and a GPU are not needed.

1. Download the frameinsight_VERSION_amd64.deb file from the official release.
2. Open it with your system's software installer and click Install.
   Your operating system may ask for your administrator password.
3. Open Applications and search for Frameinsight. Launch it.
4. Your normal browser opens the local annotation app.
5. Use Start guide for the walkthrough, or New video to begin.

If opening the .deb shows an archive viewer instead of an installer, choose
Open With > Software Install or GDebi. The terminal alternative is:
  sudo apt install ./frameinsight_VERSION_amd64.deb

The app works offline after installation. Internet is needed to check for
updates and to download an update. Installing may download normal system
dependencies. Updates are optional; annotation data stays on your computer.

CLOSING AND UPDATING
Closing the browser keeps Frameinsight running. Right-click Frameinsight in
your Applications menu and choose Close Frameinsight after saving. You can
also run: frameinsight --stop
Close it before manually installing an update. If you choose Install update
inside the app, the app closes itself and opens your system package installer.
Then reopen Frameinsight from Applications.

SAVED WORK
Data: ~/.local/share/frameinsight/data
Logs: ~/.local/state/frameinsight/server.log
These follow XDG_DATA_HOME and XDG_STATE_HOME when configured.
Updates, removal and purge keep your saved work. Back up your project and
keep original videos separately. Removing the app does not remove projects.

To copy the full data folder, first close Frameinsight. You can also use
Back up project inside the app and Restore a backup on the videos screen.

Only download packages from:
https://github.com/frameinsight/frameinsight-annotator/releases
