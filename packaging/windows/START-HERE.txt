FRAMEINSIGHT 2.0.0 — WINDOWS 11 (64-bit)

INSTALL AND OPEN
1. Open Window_setup.exe and follow the installer.
2. Double-click Frameinsight on your desktop. Your normal browser opens.
3. Check that the top-left corner says LOCAL · 2.0.0.

No terminal, Python, Node, internet connection or GPU setup is needed.
Installing an update keeps your saved work. Close Frameinsight from its
Windows tray icon before installing an update.

YOUR FIRST ANNOTATION
1. Click Start guide for a short walkthrough, or New video to begin.
2. Choose how many classes (box labels) you need and type their names.
   Continue to upload, then choose your video.
3. Once the video is ready, press N and drag a box around one person.
4. Press I to assign a person number, class and color. Select an existing
   ID when continuing a person who was already annotated.
5. Keep that person selected. Move a few frames forward, then move or
   resize their box. The app fills the frames between your corrections.
6. Go back and check those in-between frames. Correct any inaccurate box.
7. Changes save automatically. Save or Ctrl+S saves immediately.

ONE PERSON, TWO BOX TYPES
Use Visible for the part you can see. Use Extended only if you also need
to estimate the full body. Press 1 for Visible and 2 for Extended.
Both types belong to the SAME person ID. Do not press N for their second
box type. You can copy Visible boxes to Extended, then resize Extended.
Press I to set each type's class and color. Check the Editing label above
the video before making changes.

WHEN A PERSON IS HIDDEN
Delete removes the selected person's chosen box type on this frame.
Shift+Delete opens Delete boxes in range. Enter the first and last frames,
then delete. Both endpoints are included; the other box type stays.
Deleted ranges stop interpolation, even if you later draw at their ends.
To fill a range again, choose Restore deleted range, then Fill between my
boxes. Review the preview and click Remove gap & fill boxes.
Recover deleted boxes brings back original coordinates from edit history
when available. Existing boxes are kept. Ctrl+Z undoes the restoration.
No Visible box means Not visible; the reason is not automatically guessed.

REVIEW, VALIDATE AND SHARE
1. Click Finish. Generate the full annotated review video.
2. Watch and scrub through it. Slow playback and frame controls help you
   check box placement and IDs. Jump back to edit anything that is wrong.
3. Choose whether you annotated every person or selected people only.
4. Confirm your visual review, then Run annotation validation.
5. Fix any errors. Review any notes, then Prepare validated JSON.
6. Download annotations (.json). This file contains no video or images.

Automatic checks validate the saved data. They cannot decide whether a
person was missed or whether two IDs are really the same person.
Editing after a review requires a fresh review and validation.
Finished videos can be reopened. Edits return them to In progress.
Use Back up project from Help for a ZIP you can restore later. Keep your
original videos as well. Restore a backup is on the All videos screen.

HELPFUL CONTROLS
F / D = next / previous frame. Shift+F / Shift+D = jump 10 frames.
N = new person. I = person ID, class and color. Ctrl+Z = undo.
Drag inside a selected box to move it; drag an edge/corner to resize.
Scroll to zoom. Hold Space and drag to pan. Tap Space to play/pause.
Each person has Eye (hide/show), Focus (show only them), and Trash (delete).
Hiding boxes is only a viewing preference; they stay saved and exported.
Help contains the guide and shortcuts. The right panel also shows commands.

CLOSE, UPDATE AND BACK UP
Closing the browser leaves Frameinsight running. Right-click its icon
near the Windows clock and choose Exit Frameinsight after saving.
Data: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Frameinsight\Data
Logs: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Frameinsight\Logs\server.log
Uninstalling keeps saved work. You can also back up the entire data folder
while Frameinsight is closed. The installer is unsigned, so Windows may
show an unknown-publisher warning.

To remove old work, open All videos and click Delete video under its card.
Confirm the filename, then Delete video and annotations. This cannot be
undone. Original files and downloaded exports are not deleted.
