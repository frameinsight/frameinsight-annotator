FRAMEINSIGHT — WINDOWS 11 (64-bit)

Install Window_setup.exe, then double-click Frameinsight on your desktop. Your normal browser will open.
No terminal, Python, Node, internet connection or GPU setup is needed.

1. The opening screen shows your videos. Click one to continue your work.
2. For a new recording, click New video. Choose the number of classes and
   type their names. Click Continue to upload, then select your video.
3. When the video is ready, press N and drag a box around a person.
4. Press I to assign a number, choose or create a class, and choose a color.
   For an existing person, choose their ID from the list.
5. Skip some frames and draw/adjust the box for the SAME person. The app
   fills the frames between your boxes. Check these and correct any mistakes.
6. Changes save automatically. You can also click Save or press Ctrl+S.
7. When everyone is annotated and tracked, click Finish. Confirm that you
   checked every person, then choose an export and prepare your download.
   Annotations JSON contains no video or images.

F = next frame. D = previous frame. Shift+F / Shift+D = jump 10 frames.
N = new person. I = choose ID, class and color. Ctrl+Z = undo.
Drag inside a selected box to move it; drag its edge/corner to resize.
Box present = Visible. No box = Not visible, automatically.
Press Delete to remove the selected person's box on this frame.
For several frames, press Shift+Delete, enter the first and last frame,
then click Delete boxes. Deleted frames will not be filled by interpolation.
Draw a box again to make the person visible; no G/H steps are needed.
Ctrl+Z restores a deletion. The cause of missing boxes remains unspecified.
Each person has Eye (hide/show), Focus (show only them), and Trash (delete).
Hidden boxes stay saved and exported. Ctrl+Z restores a deleted person.
Useful shortcuts are shown on the right side of the editor.

You can reopen finished videos. Editing changes their status to In progress.
Closing the browser leaves Frameinsight running. Right-click its icon
near the Windows clock and choose Exit Frameinsight after saving.

Data: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Frameinsight\Data
Logs: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Frameinsight\Logs\server.log
Updating or uninstalling keeps your saved work. Keep the original videos
and back up your data directory while the app is closed.
The installer is unsigned; Windows may show an unknown-publisher warning.

To remove old work, open All videos and click Delete video under its card.
Confirm the filename, then Delete video and annotations. This cannot be undone.
Your original file and downloaded exports are not deleted.
